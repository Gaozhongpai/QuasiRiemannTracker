\section{Statement and fixed geometry}
The locally checked theorem is: for every positive modulus $n$, every Dirichlet
character $\chi$ modulo $n$, and every $s\in\mathbb C$,
\[
 \Re s>\tht,\qquad \neg(\chi=1\text{ and }s=1)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad L(\chi,s)\ne0.                 \tag{1}
\]
The OpenAI--Collet framework reduces (1) to improving the Eisenstein
Hecke-family global zero supremum $\beta$ \cite{openai,collet}.
We use the accepted $\beta\le21871/25000$ as an incumbent input,
never the new conclusion $\beta\le\tht$.

The new rational geometry is
\begin{align*}
 h&=\frac{3181}{4000},&d_-&=\frac{15893}{20000},&d_+&=h+10^{-10},\\
 \ell&=\frac{11}{3}-4\tht=\frac{31373}{187500},&
 x&=1+\ell-h=\frac{558109}{1500000},&
 y&=h-2\ell=\frac{690907}{1500000},\\
 \kap_*&=2\tht-1=\frac{93709}{125000},&
 \lambda&=\frac{19793}{5000},&B&=\frac{987047}{1000000}.
\end{align*}
The actual initial affine moment is proved on
\[
 \frac{41}{100}\le m_1,m_2\le\frac{11}{25},\qquad
 \frac{17}{500}\le b\le\frac1{25},\qquad
 m_1+m_2+\lambda b\le B.                              \tag{2}
\]
For equal lengths this is $2m+\lambda b\le B$. The literal energy,
masks, selected primes, seminorms and height losses are preserved;
the constants precede the moving row and original slot subset.

\section{LOW: use the proved incumbent without circular feedback}
Put $q=839/1250$, $\kap_0=9371/12500$, and $r_L=1/4000000$.
Here $\kap_0=2(0.874840)-1$ is fixed by the predecessor. For a cell upper endpoint $b$, set

             +\frac{r_L}{1-q/\kap_0},                 \tag{3}\\

The exact identity
\[
 a(b)-\frac{q}{6\kap_0}\bigl(D(b)+6a(b)\bigr)=r_L       \tag{4}

pays the complete LOW removal cost: both the capacity and its removal price
change with the incumbent. The accepted positive/zero energy pair supplies
all heights and masks. The stronger target is weakened to Collet's generic
LOW fee ledger for the outer contraction.

\section{HIGH: retain actual width and the common-allocation geometry}
The marked HIGH source reuses timbalud's asymmetric inverse/plain counting
and common-radical architecture \cite{asymmetric}. The actual first numerator
also satisfies a valuation bound whose logarithmic form is
$4L_c\le5(c+d)$. Together with the original radical and cancellation gates,
it gives a common-width rate
\[
 \rho(\delta)=1-\frac{10\delta}{3(5-2\delta)},\qquad
 \gamma(\delta)=\delta-
       \max\left(4\rho(\delta)-1,\frac{2+22\rho(\delta)}{21}\right). \tag{5}
\]
The second allocation pays rate $\delta$. Neither reflection orientation
changes the first-numerator identity or substitutes a synthetic source norm.

Write $u,f,v\ge0$ for the removed common, frozen and surviving widths.
The two actual allocation stages and the tiny-slot discard prove
\[
 u+f+v\ge S(b):=b-10^{-5}-10^{-3},\qquad
 Q\ge F(b)-2u,\quad
 F(b)=\frac{122}{125}\bigl(1-6a(b)-10^{-3}\bigr).       \tag{6}
\]
The crucial change is the moving quantity $S(b)$: the original selected
width is retained through both allocations, before choosing the cell.
Replacing it everywhere by the smallest permitted width loses useful
payment at larger cells, where $a(b)$ and $A(b)$ also move.

Set
\[
 r(\delta)=\frac{32}{39}\frac{\delta(5-6\delta)}{5-2\delta},
 \qquad t(\delta)=\frac{6\rho(\delta)-1}{250},\qquad
 \tau_s=\frac{13}{400}.
\]
The marked payment is
$\gamma u+\delta f+r\min(v,\tau_sQ)$; its large sixth-power tail uses
$\gamma u+\delta f+tQ$.
If $v\le\tau_sQ$, compare the three width rates and use the first inequality
in (6). If $v>\tau_sQ$, insert the second inequality in (6) and use
$2r\tau_s\le\gamma$. The analogous tail step uses $2t\le\gamma$.
Consequently it suffices to check four scalar channels. With
\[
 H(b,\delta)=1-\rho(\delta)-\delta A(b)
                         -a(b)(5-6\rho(\delta)),
\]
we display a conservative common ledger charging $1/20000$ and a separate
$1/200000$ raw-root allowance:
\begin{align*}
 H+\gamma S-\tfrac1{20000}-\tfrac1{200000}&\ge10^{-6},&
 H+rS-\tfrac1{20000}-\tfrac1{200000}&\ge10^{-6},\\
 H+r\tau_sF-\tfrac1{20000}-\tfrac1{200000}&\ge10^{-6},&
 H+tF-\tfrac1{20000}-\tfrac1{200000}&\ge10^{-6}.
\end{align*}
The marked channels reserve the latter charge before the raw-root estimate;
the unmarked common-width channel has the stronger bound without that charge.
These hold on the full moving cell range
$3401/100000\le b\le1/25$ and $q\le\delta\le7/10$.
Monotonicity pays $7/10\le\delta\le3/4$ directly.
The actual selected-family, physical-fiber and all-height suppliers preserve
their original coefficients, frequency collisions and finite dictionaries.

\section{ERROR: preserve the separate deletion capacity}
The improved LOW incumbent does not change the original error-deletion
capacity. Its defect is
\[
 D_E(b)=B-1+(9/2-\lambda)b,                            \tag{7}
\]
with $9/2=6(3/4)$. In particular, the first error branch has the independent
uniform reserve
\[
 \frac{a(b)}4-\frac{D_E(b)}6\ge\frac1{1000}.            \tag{8}
\]
The sixth and seventh branches have separately proved positive reserves;
all three exceed the pre-loss fee-ledger reserve $7/30000000$.
Absorbing branch losses halves this to $7/60000000$ in the complete
normalized physical sum: its saving term is $-M(7/60000000)$, with $M$
the source scale and allocation/shell losses still displayed separately.
The source proof includes the full retained radical witness and the
moving-prime average exactly once. The normalized ERROR family is converted
to physical paid terms before it joins the actual MAIN family.
Zero and tail terms use the new $a(b),A(b)$ as well: for example,
$1-A(b)-5a(b)\ge227876729/3446640000$ and the full amplifier coefficient
is at most $2048$. Thus a main-only scalar improvement is not being passed
off as a complete source improvement.

\section{Use the new moment inside the actual detector count}
DaysSky's construction applies an initial affine moment separately to each
actual detector dictionary character \cite{detector}. We keep this order.
For inverse length $r$, plain length $m$, cutoff $t$ with $r+m=t$,
zero density $\delta$ and normalized amplitude $p$, the three count inputs are
\begin{align*}
 I&=1-\delta\bigl(p+(1-p)r\bigr),\\
 P&=1-2\delta m-2\delta p\frac{B-2m}{\lambda},\\
 L&=1-\delta+(5/6-\delta)(t-1).
\end{align*}
Balancing these gives, with positive denominators,
\begin{align*}
 D(p)&=3-p-4p/\lambda,&a_p&=2-4p/\lambda,&c_p&=1-2pB/\lambda,\\
 J(\delta,p)&=(5/6-\delta)D(p)+\delta(1-p)a_p,\\
 t_N&=1+\frac{\delta(1-p)c_p}{J(\delta,p)},&
 \Raff&=1-\delta+
       \frac{(5/6-\delta)\delta(1-p)c_p}{J(\delta,p)}.   \tag{9}
\end{align*}
The selected width must stay in the proved window of (2), including the
rounding subtraction. The detector checks this with $m\le847/2000$ and
$1\le t_N\le3/2$. Smaller widths or inadmissible plain lengths retain the
original inverse/plain estimates. The global count uses three regimes:

Regime & Density interval & Amplitude interval\\\midrule

Accepted affine fallback & $[89/200,451/1000]$ & $[199/400,1/2]$\\

\end{tabular}

The shared inverse/prime moment constants and balanced count parameters
are fixed once. The new line also supplies the accepted fallback window,

The amplitude-class theorem consumes this same actual count.

The finite detector dictionary is formed after the final target character

\section{Weighted numerator and whole-domain certificates}
Akash Levy's weighted-numerator architecture and Collet's full continuation
transport are retained \cite{akash,collet}. The source line changes the
available numerator capacity on the upper height interval to
\[
 C_N(d)=\frac{2y-(2-B)d}{\lambda}.
\]
On the lower interval it remains
$C_O(d)=\min(d,2y-d)/(6\kap_*)$.
The physical shifted capacity is used with switch
$t_0=103/100000$; this is distinct from the source frequency allowance
$10^{-3}$. The choice pays the literal large-shift gate and preserves the
unclipped physical slot cap $37/1000$.

For either capacity $C$ the normalized critical row is
\begin{align*}
 E(d,C,\delta,p)={}&\frac{1+\delta}{2}+\frac{13h}{75}
 -\frac y2-\frac\ell2+\ell\delta p
 +d\left(\frac14-\frac{17}{50}\right)
 -\frac{\delta pC}{2}+\frac{3d}{4}R(\kap_*,\delta,p).  \tag{10}
\end{align*}
Exact rational Bernstein certificates prove $E\le\tht-10^{-7}$ over all
$0\le\delta\le3/4$, $0\le p\le1/2$. They cover the three count regimes,
not just a sampled critical point. Affinity in $d$ reduces the two full
height intervals to five ends: $d_0=1/200$, $y$, $d_-$ with $C_O$, and
$d_-,d_+$ with $C_N$. Positivity of the count denominators, source geometry,
selected-width and tail gates, amplitude monotonicity and the varying
$\kap=2\beta-1$ feedback are proved separately and consumed by the row provider.

\section{Actual assembly, verification scope and attribution}
The source, detector and numerator components are joined through the
following literal contracts:
\begin{gather*}
 \text{actual LOW/HIGH/ERROR}\ \longrightarrow\ \text{full geometry budget}
 \ \longrightarrow\ \text{initial affine moment}\\
 \longrightarrow\ \text{dictionary moment/count}
 \ \longrightarrow\ \text{finite weighted numerator}
 \ \longrightarrow\ \text{common-probe continuation}.  \tag{11}
\end{gather*}
The full geometry budget includes both reflections, shifted Mellin heights,
zero/tail terms and the original retained ERROR witness. The initial moment
has the quantifiers of the original source theorem, with the new numerical
line. The finite numerator uses the new physical lengths and the actual
source and count receipts. Both lower and upper estimates are for the same
normalized probe; the inherited correction factor is analytic and nonzero
on the required new half-plane.

The continuation argument keeps $\beta$ as the global supremum. Under a
contradiction assumption $\beta>\tht$, the common positive continuation
reserve is chosen before the primitive target characters. Target-dependent
heights may then be chosen, including the finite dictionary maximum above.
The resulting common gap improves the global supremum. This quantifier order
is essential: a target-by-target gap without a common positive reserve would
not establish the supremum contradiction.
